DFS Lineup Intelligence Engine Specification

Objective

Build a professional DFS lineup generation engine capable of producing Cash, Small-Field GPP, Large-Field GPP, Single Entry, 3-Max, and MME (Mass Multi-Entry) lineups for DraftKings, FanDuel, PrizePicks-style contests, and future sportsbook applications.

The goal is not simply to maximize projected points.

The goal is to maximize Expected Value (EV), leverage ownership inefficiencies, capture correlation, and create contest-specific lineups that can realistically finish in the top 1% of tournaments.

⸻

Core Philosophy

Most DFS players ask:

“Who scores the most fantasy points?”

Professional DFS players ask:

“What lineup wins if my assumptions about the slate are correct?”

The system should be built around scenario-based outcomes rather than raw projections.

Core Scoring Framework

Every player receives six independent scores.

1. Projection Score

Measures:

* Raw projection
* Fantasy points
* Fantasy points per minute
* Expected fantasy output

Inputs:

* Model projections
* Market projections
* Historical averages

⸻

2. Ceiling Score

Measures:

Player’s realistic 90th percentile outcome.

Inputs:

* Historical ceiling games
* Usage spikes
* Game environment
* Matchup

Questions:

Can this player score 2x expectation?

Can this player break the slate?

⸻

3. Ownership Leverage Score

Measures:

Difference between projection and ownership.

Formula Example:

Leverage = Ceiling × (1 - Ownership)

Examples:

Player A:
40 Projection
35% Ownership

Player B:
38 Projection
5% Ownership

Player B receives a higher leverage score.

⸻

4. Correlation Score

Measures lineup synergy.

Positive Examples:

NFL:

* QB + WR
* QB + TE
* QB + WR + WR
* Game Bring-Back

NBA:

* Same Game Stack
* Close Competitive Games

MLB:

* Team Stacks
* Full Offensive Stacks

Negative Examples:

* RB vs Opposing Defense
* Multiple Players Cannibalizing Usage
* Opposing Closers

⸻

5. Role Stability Score

Measures consistency of opportunity.

Inputs:

* Minutes
* Usage
* Touches
* Snap Share
* Target Share
* Rotation Stability

Questions:

Will this player receive the expected opportunity?

⸻

6. Volatility Score

Measures outcome variance.

Inputs:

* Standard deviation
* Historical consistency
* Ceiling/Floor spread

Applications:

Cash:
Prefer lower volatility.

GPP:
Prefer controlled volatility.

Ultimate Goal

The system should not simply build lineups.

The system should think like a professional DFS player.

Every lineup should be evaluated through:

1. Projection
2. Ceiling
3. Ownership
4. Correlation
5. Leverage
6. Contest Context

The objective is to maximize long-term Expected Value and generate lineups capable of competing for first place rather than merely cashing.
